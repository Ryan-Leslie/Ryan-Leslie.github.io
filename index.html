About Me

I am a 17-year-old student at Patrick Henry High School in San Diego, pursuing a pathway toward a major and career in Nursing. Driven by a deep commitment to patient care and community wellness, I have dedicated over 115 hours volunteering in the nursing units at Kaiser Hospital — spending time with patients through conversation, providing basic care items, playing games with them, and assisting nurses with discharges and room preparation.

Currently working toward: graduating Patrick Henry High School in 2027 and earning acceptance into nursing school to begin my career as a healthcare professional.
